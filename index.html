☁ Climate & Climate Change

Temperature trends, rainfall patterns, sea level scenarios, and delta geography.

1. Climate of Bangladesh

Bangladesh features a tropical monsoon climate driven by seasonal wind reversals and heavy precipitation.

Rainfall distribution is highly variable across both spatial regions and seasonal timings, with the bulk arriving during monsoon months.

Temperature and rainfall changes directly affect agriculture, fisheries, livestock, freshwater availability, settlements, and natural ecosystems.

2. Temperature Shifts

Projections show warming across all seasons, with winter warming often projected to be proportionately higher.

Projected Temperature Rise: +1.0°C by 2030

Long-term Target Scenario: Up to +2.4°C by 2100

3. Rainfall Patterns

Climate models consistently forecast an increase in monsoon rainfall volume and intensity.

Monsoon Rainfall Projection: +5% to +12% increase by mid-century.

In contrast, winter precipitation is expected to show little change or a slight decrease, heightening dry season moisture stress.

4. Sea-Level Rise & Delta Topography

Predominantly a low-lying deltaic nation, much of Bangladesh consists of flat, low-elevation river floodplains.

National scenarios estimate a sea level rise of tens of centimetres during 2050–2100.

Impacts include intensified coastal inundation, inland salinity migration, and river drainage congestion via the backwater effect.

🌀 Cyclones & Storm Surges

1991 Cyclone: US$1.78 Billion damage; 247,000 tons cereal crop lost.

Nov 2007 (Sidr): ~3,500 lives lost.

🌄 Droughts, Landslides & Earthquakes

Drought & Salinity: Mainly impacts northwestern regions. Sea-level rise forces seawater up the Meghna Estuary, corrupting freshwater aquifers.
June 2007 Landslide: Triggered by extreme rainfall in Chittagong, resulting in 128 deaths and over 8,000 displaced.
Seismic Risk: Subduction zone beneath the Indo-Burman Ranges has potential for an earthquake exceeding 8.2 Magnitude, affecting ~140 Million people.

↘ Environmental Impacts

Agriculture, fisheries, livestock, and health sector risks.

🌾 Agriculture & Food Security

Floods disrupt transplanting schedules and reduce Aman crop acreage.

Salinity and drought decrease yields across Aus, Aman, Boro rice and wheat.

Heat and moisture stress disproportionately affect wheat and various Rabi crops during dry months.

🐟 Fisheries & Aquaculture

Flooding breaches river embankments, causing pond washouts and escape of cultured stock.

Storm surges destroy coastal shrimp gher enclosures.

Water temperatures above 32°C lead to elevated shrimp-fry mortality and trigger toxic algal blooms.

🐄 Livestock Health

Cyclones and extended floods cause direct mortality and disease outbreaks (e.g., anthrax, foot-and-mouth).

Drought reduces fodder and drinking water availability, diminishing milk output and draught capacity.

🏥 Human Health Risks

Outbreaks of cholera, diarrheal diseases, dengue, and malaria linked to flooded water contamination and stagnant mosquito breeding sites.

Increased incidence of extreme heat stress, mental health trauma, and physical injuries following major disasters.

🏘 Health, Settlements & Livelihoods

Infrastructure damage, asset loss, and ecological displacement.

🏗 Infrastructure Damage

Recurrent floods and cyclones damage critical arterial roads, bridges, embankments, educational institutions, commercial markets, health centers, and telecommunication lines—leading to elevated maintenance overheads and economic disruptions.

⛵ Livelihoods & Ecological Migration

Resource-dependent livelihoods in agriculture, fisheries, and day labor endure severe asset shocks. Repeated property losses force vulnerable households into cycle debt and drive rural-to-urban displacement, creating "ecological refugees".

◈ Adaptation & Management

Targeted strategic interventions across sectors.

🌊 Water & Floods

  • Re-excavate rivers & khals
  • Dredging & drainage structure repair
  • Flood-proof critical infrastructure
  • Community-based warning systems

☀️ Drought & Salinity

  • Re-excavate ponds & reservoirs
  • Surface & groundwater conjunctive use
  • Push salinity front seaward with dry-season flow
  • Desalination & saline-free drinking water

🌱 Agriculture & Health

  • Saline, flood, drought tolerant crop strains
  • Cropping calendar adjustments
  • Vector control & water treatment capacity
  • Target poor community health infrastructure

◎ Disaster Preparedness Framework

Governmental bodies, legal frameworks, and early warning technology.

🏛 Institutional Structure

Ministry of Disaster Management & Relief: Established in 1972 for policy & budget allocation.
National Disaster Management Council (NDMC): Apex leadership headed directly by the Prime Minister.

📡 Early Warning & Volunteer Force

Cyclone Preparedness Programme (CPP): 65,000+ trained volunteers.
SERVIR Satellite System: Successfully achieved flood forecasting 8 days in advance across 9 basin locations in 2014.
